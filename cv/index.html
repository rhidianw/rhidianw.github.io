Data Engineer · ETL, warehousing and multi-channel reconciliation

Profile

Data engineer working across the full path from source system to decision: ingestion, warehousing, modelling and the reporting layer on top. My work sits in multi-channel consumer goods, where the same product exists simultaneously in an ERP, several marketplaces, an ecommerce platform and a third-party logistics system — each with its own identifiers, its own definition of availability, and no agreement between them.

I'm strongest at the integration end: making unreliable feeds land reliably, resolving identity between systems that were never designed to talk to each other, and building models that stay correct when a source system changes shape without warning. I care more about a number being defensible than a dashboard being pretty.

Experience

Data Engineer 20XX – present
Nexus Brands Group

Part of a small central data team supporting the group's operating companies, each selling physical products across its own mix of direct, marketplace and wholesale channels.

  • Build and maintain the group reporting warehouse on MariaDB — the single source that downstream sales, stock, margin and catalogue reporting is built on.
  • Own the scheduled ETL estate: Python jobs on a Linux host that pull from the ERP, marketplace APIs, an integration platform and partner flat-file drops, landing to a columnar staging layer before load so failed runs can be replayed without re-hitting source systems.
  • Design the modelling layer that reconciles identity and definitions across those systems — which identifier is authoritative, what counts as sellable stock, and which fields have quietly stopped being maintained.
  • Re-modelled marketplace fulfilment inventory after establishing that stock pools at the barcode level rather than the SKU level, removing a substantial systematic overstatement from reported stock cover.
  • Built catalogue reconciliation between the ERP and live marketplace and storefront listings, surfacing listings attributed to the wrong seller account and lines that were discontinued in one system but still selling in another.
  • Integrated subscription and replenishment data from a marketplace API with non-standard pagination, including a pre-save transform to normalise discount tiers before the feed lands.
  • Built product compliance screening that cross-references catalogue attributes against dangerous-goods declarations, flagging mismatches before the marketplace finds them and suppresses the listing.
  • Work directly with buying, brand and operations teams to turn ambiguous questions into defined, repeatable measures.
