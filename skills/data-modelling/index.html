Data modelling

✦ ✧ ✦

What it is

Deciding how data should be structured: what the tables are, what one row means in each, and how they relate. Relational modelling organises for correctness; dimensional modelling organises facts and dimensions for analysis.

Where I use it

The part of the job I would defend as the most valuable. Deciding what counts as sellable stock, which identifier is authoritative when two systems disagree, and what the grain of a table actually is.

The bit people get wrong

That it is upfront paperwork you can skip to get to the dashboard. Every shortcut here surfaces later as numbers that do not reconcile, and by then the wrong definition is embedded in a dozen reports people already trust.
