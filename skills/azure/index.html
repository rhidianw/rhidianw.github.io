Azure pipelines

✦ ✧ ✦

What it is

Microsoft's cloud platform for orchestrating data movement — scheduling jobs, moving data between services and managing the plumbing between systems, without running the servers yourself.

Where I use it

Part of the data platform work alongside Snowflake and Databricks. Exposure rather than deep specialism, and I would rather be straight about that than inflate it.

The bit people get wrong

That managed orchestration removes the need to understand what is being orchestrated. It removes the need to maintain the scheduler. When a job fails at 3am, you still need to know exactly what it was doing.
