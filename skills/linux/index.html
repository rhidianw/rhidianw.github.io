Linux

✦ ✧ ✦

What it is

The operating system almost all servers run. Working in it means being comfortable at a command line — moving around a filesystem, reading logs, managing processes, and understanding who is permitted to do what.

Where I use it

Everything scheduled runs on a Linux host. Which means the job includes the unglamorous parts: file permissions, cron, disk space, and working out why something that runs fine by hand fails under a scheduler.

The bit people get wrong

That permissions are an afterthought. A pipeline that writes files as one user and gets read by another will work perfectly until the day it does not, and the error will have nothing to do with your code.
