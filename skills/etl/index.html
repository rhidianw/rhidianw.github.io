ETL & pipeline design

✦ ✧ ✦

What it is

Extract, Transform, Load — getting data out of source systems, reshaping it, and putting it somewhere it can be used. ELT reverses the last two, loading raw and transforming in the warehouse. The arguments about which letter goes where are less interesting than they sound.

Where I use it

This is the spine of my job. Scheduled jobs pulling from an ERP, marketplace APIs, an integration platform and partner file drops, landing to staging, then loading into the warehouse.

The bit people get wrong

That the hard part is moving the data. The hard part is what happens when it goes wrong at 3am — whether a half-finished run leaves the warehouse in a broken state, and whether you can simply run it again. Pipelines should be rerunnable without fear. Most of the design effort goes there.
