Why darts

Because it is the only sport where the arithmetic is the hard part. Anyone can learn to put a dart roughly where they want it. Working out, under pressure, in front of a crowd, that 141 goes treble twenty, treble fifteen, double eighteen — and then actually hitting it — is a different thing entirely.

It is also a sport of almost perfect legibility. There is no formation to misread and no tactical phase to explain. A man throws a small object at a number. Either it goes in or it does not, and everybody in the building knows which immediately.

I came for the maths and stayed for the walk-ons.
